### 📖 &nbsp;Current Research — *Quantum Cryptography & Secure Key Exchange*

> Investigating secure communication methods grounded in **Quantum Key Distribution (QKD)**, analysing how quantum cryptographic techniques outperform classical approaches in key exchange security. The work spans the **mathematical foundations of quantum cryptography**, protocol simulation via **IBM Qiskit**, and rigorous **QBER-based security threshold analysis** of the BB84 protocol.

<details>
<summary><b>📋 Key Research Activities</b></summary>
<br/>

- In-depth study of classical cryptographic key exchange mechanisms (DH, RSA, ECDH)
- Formal analysis of the BB84 quantum key distribution protocol
- Side-by-side security comparison: classical vs. quantum cryptographic models
- Eavesdropping simulation: intercept-resend attack with QBER impact measurement
- Post-quantum threat modelling against harvest-now-decrypt-later attacks
- Literature review & manuscript preparation for peer-reviewed publication

</details>
